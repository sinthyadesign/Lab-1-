# UX220- My Favorite Thing I Have Written 
Hey, I'm Sinthya Sivarasa. If you ask me where I see myself five years after finishing my degree in UX Design at Laurier Brantford, I picture chatting with an old classmate and telling them I landed my first UX job. I want to look back and actually feel proud—knowing I built a solid portfolio and tackled real projects that made technology easier for people.

In those five years, I plan to get hands-on with everything from UX research to wireframing, prototyping to usability testing. I also want to get better at speaking up, working with different teams, and making sure my ideas come across clearly. Honestly, I can’t think of anything better than looking back and seeing how the stuff I learned in university set me up, not just for a job, but to really grow into this industry

In the first five years after graduation, one thing I really want is to stand on my own two feet financially. I’m aiming for a steady income, a solid savings account, and the guts to actually handle my money without second-guessing myself.

I’d love to own my own car, too. It’d just make life simpler commuting, traveling, all of it. No more juggling rides or waiting around for someone else. Handling all my bills, tucking some money away for the future, and even being able to help my family out when they need it—that’s the kind of independence I’m after. Reaching that point would really show me how far I’ve come since graduation.

Family really matters to me. Even five years from now, I want to tell my friends that I stayed close with the people who count, no matter how hectic things get. Sure, I’ll be working hard and following my own dreams, but I still want to show up for my family.

I hope I’ll remember the birthdays we celebrated, the quiet nights we spent together, and all the new things we tried as a family. I want to be someone they can rely on, not just someone lost in their own world. If I keep growing but manage to keep my family close, that’ll mean I’m doing something right.

I don’t want the first five years after graduation to be all about work. I want to get out and see new places, hang out with my friends and family, and keep doing things that let me be creative.

I’m also hoping to grow Decor Blossom, my little flower business where I put together bouquets and decorate custom bottles. There’s just something great about making personalized stuff and seeing people light up when they get it. In five years, I want Decor Blossom to be bigger, but I still want it to be something I truly love, not just a job. Really, I hope I’ll look back and know I made time for hobbies, relationships, and new experiences, all while pushing toward my career goals.
